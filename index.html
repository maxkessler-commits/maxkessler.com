Based in New York · 12:00 PM

I took the long way into product design. I started out designing furniture, which taught me to care about how things feel to use, then moved through agencies like frog and Smart Design and in-house teams at Google and Instagram.

Today I lead a UX team at YouTube focused on creators, from AI provenance to helping them reach audiences in any language. It's work without obvious answers or a single owner, so I spend much of my time with product, engineering, research and policy, turning ambiguity into direction. I stay close to the craft, and I care just as much about growing the designers on my team.
